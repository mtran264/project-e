# Project E Inventory Manager

A single-file, offline-capable inventory, sales, returns, expenses, and preorder management tool.

> **This is a personal project.** It was built to help me get rid of excess inventory and shared as a work in progress. It is not a commercial product, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by eBay, Mercari, Poshmark, Depop, Etsy, Swappa, or any other marketplace or company named in the app.

## What it does

- Manage inventory, sales, and expenses
- Summarize inventory items and provide insights on other metrics
- Export individual reports as CSV files and back up or restore your data as a JSON file

## Your data stays on your device

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, login, or user profile.
- **No servers.** The app does not send your inventory, sales, expense, or customer information anywhere.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking code.
- **Local storage only.** Your records are saved in your browser on the device you use. Clearing your browser data, or using the in-app "Clear all data" option, removes them.
- **You control backups.** Export a JSON backup from Settings and keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a backup overwrites the data currently on that device.

### No network requests

The app is a single HTML file with no external scripts, fonts, or images. It does not connect to the internet while you use it. Exports are created on your device and saved directly to your downloads folder.

**Keep exports private.** CSV reports and JSON backups contain your records, including customer names, order details, and business figures. Store them carefully and don't post them publicly.

## Getting started

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).
2. Optional: on a phone or tablet, use the browser's "Add to Home Screen" option to open it like an app.
3. Choose **Load sample data** to explore, or start entering your own items. Sample data is fictional.

To remove the sample data, use **Settings > Clear all data**. Clearing data cannot be undone, so export a backup first.
